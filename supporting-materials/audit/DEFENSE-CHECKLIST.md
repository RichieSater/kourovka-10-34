# Sole-author defense checklist

This checklist is separate from the theorem-correctness gates. It closes only
after the author can reconstruct the proof unaided and an independent mock
examiner records a satisfactory assessment. AI-generated answers do not close
this row.

## Required reconstruction topics

- [ ] Minimal counterexample and the socle `S^k`.
- [ ] Coordinate closure and `t | x^k k!`.
- [ ] Construction and exact order of `B_V`.
- [ ] Normal saturation in the maximality argument.
- [ ] Nonconjugacy from distinct stable classes.
- [ ] The valuation contradiction for every `k`.
- [ ] Primitive-prime avoidance for both selected Levi factors.
- [ ] Graph and graph-field automorphisms.
- [ ] The logical boundary of finite computations and regression tests.
- [ ] Complete CFSG-family and exception coverage.

## Required counterfactual questions

- [ ] What fails if `V` is not normally saturating?
- [ ] What fails if the class is not `X`-stable?
- [ ] Why can unique maximal-subgroup order prove stability in the applicable
      sporadic cases, and when is it insufficient?
- [ ] Why do 7,892 passing parameters not prove an infinite-family theorem?
- [ ] Why does the `k!` term not defeat the valuation bound?
