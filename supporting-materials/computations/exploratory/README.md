# Historical exploratory artifacts (not evidence)

Nothing in this directory is part of either binary proof gate or any current
manuscript claim.  The files are retained only to document development history
and are excluded from `computations/certificates/SHA256SUMS` and
`audit/EVIDENCE.sha256`.

`legacy-scripts/` and `legacy-logs/` contain the early direct searches,
prefilters, probes, and consistency runs (sweeps A--I).  They include two
important historical limitations:

- `sweepB_perfect.log` terminates in a GAP error; the later
  `sweepB2_perfect.log` completed its intended redundant search.
- the two sweep-G logs tested only 20 of 34 candidate top groups over
  `PSU(3,5)^3`.

Neither statement affects the proof: the current manuscript makes no empirical
consistency claim and excludes each relevant socle uniformly through the
proof-essential J/K certificates.  Do not cite any file here as a certificate.
